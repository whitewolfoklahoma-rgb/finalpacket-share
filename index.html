About these clocks. Oklahoma's death-certificate steps are set by 63 O.S. § 1-317 (2025 text, as amended effective November 1, 2024): the certificate is filed within 10 calendar days of the death; the funeral director transmits the partial certificate to the medical certifier within 24 hours; the certifier completes the medical certification within 5 calendar days of receiving it; and the funeral director reviews, signs, and submits within 4 calendar days of receiving the medical certification. Please note: the State Department of Health's public web page still describes a 3-day filing period from the earlier text of the law. Confirm the period your registrar expects — the clocks here follow the current statute text. VERIFY against the current statute and your registrar before relying on any date shown.

Help

Plain answers to the things this program does.

Starting a case

Choose New case on the Cases screen and fill in the case file. The fields marked * are the facts the packet cannot be completed without. Save the case — you will see the saved total on screen. You can come back and add facts at any time; every document is drawn from the same record.

Printing the packet

Open the Packet screen and choose the case. Each document shows whether it is ready and names any fact still missing. Print the ready documents prints the complete ones together. A document with missing facts can be printed on its own, and it will say plainly what is missing.

The family information form

The Family form screen shows the form the family completes in the full service — one entry of the facts, before they arrive, with no account for them to create. What they enter fills the case file directly.

The custody log

Choose the case on the Custody log screen and add an entry each time the decedent is received, transferred, or released. The log is kept in the order entries are recorded and is printed with the packet.

The filing clocks

The Filings screen shows where each filing stands and the date each step is due, with the law each date comes from. When you have filed or sent something yourself, record the date on the case file — or use the button on the Filings screen — so the clocks stay true.

What FinalPacket does not do

FinalPacket prepares worksheets and forms. It does not file a death certificate, does not connect to the state's electronic death registration system, and does not submit anything to Social Security, the VA, or any other agency. You review, sign, and file every document. The documents it prints are preparation worksheets, labeled as demo renderings in this demonstration — not official forms.

Where your data lives

Your data lives on this device, in this browser, and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded. Back it up from the Settings screen — export a copy and keep it somewhere safe, because clearing the browser's data will clear these records.

Rules this demo follows, and where they came from.
· Death certificate clocks: 63 O.S. § 1-317 (2025 text) — filed within 10 calendar days of death; partial certificate to the certifier within 24 hours; medical certification within 5 calendar days of the certifier receiving it; director's review, signature, and submission within 4 calendar days of receiving the medical certification. The State Department of Health's public page still describes the earlier 3-day period — VERIFY with your registrar.
· SSA-721: Form SSA-721 (10-2024), "Statement of Death by Funeral Director," OMB No. 0960-0142. Social Security advises the form need not be sent when the death is reported through Electronic Death Registration, and that pages 2–3 of the official form are given to survivors.
· VA burial claim: VA Form 21P-530EZ (Oct 2024), "Application for Burial Benefits (Under 38 U.S.C. Chapter 23)." A claim for the non-service-connected burial allowance must reach VA within 2 years of the burial or cremation. Benefit amounts are not shown in this program — they change, and the current figures should be checked on VA's published table. VERIFY.
· Cremation authorization wording and any waiting period: not stated here, because they depend on current Oklahoma law and your crematory's requirements. VERIFY before relying on the worksheet.
· Contract: your firm's agreement must satisfy the FTC Funeral Rule and state law. The packet's statement of selections is a worksheet, not that contract. VERIFY.
